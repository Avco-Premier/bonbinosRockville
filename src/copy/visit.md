## Seating and groups

We don't take reservations, so every table is first come, first served. Good for kids.

## Ordering ahead and delivery

[Order ahead online](https://bonbinospizza-rockville.direct-ordering.com) and pick up at the restaurant. We deliver within about 3 miles for a $3 fee, with a $1 minimum. [Order online](https://bonbinospizza-rockville.direct-ordering.com) or call (516) 764-9237.

## Amenities and payment

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We don't have outdoor seating, and dogs aren't allowed. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.

## What to order

**Bonbino's Special Pizza**: Bonbino's Special Pizza costs $29.95. It's made with sausage, pepperoni, peppers, onions, mushrooms, meatballs and and extra cheese.

**Roman Stuffed Sicilian Pizza**: The Roman Stuffed Sicilian Pizza costs $29. It's made with potato, cheese, sausage, pepperoni and onions.
