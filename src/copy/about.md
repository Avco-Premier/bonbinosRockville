Bonbino's is a pizza restaurant at 605 Merrick Rd in Rockville Centre, NY. You'll find us near CVS and Oceanside School 5. Come in for lunch and dinner any day of the week.

The pizza by the slice list runs 13 choices long, from the Penne Alla Vodka Pizza Slice to the Cheese Pizza Slice. There's also a pizza list with four choices and a stuffed Sicilian pizza list with two choices. Bonbino's Special Pizza ($29.95) is made with sausage, pepperoni, peppers, onions, mushrooms, meatballs, and extra cheese. The Roman Stuffed Sicilian Pizza ($29) is made with potato, cheese, sausage, pepperoni, onions. For guests who avoid gluten, the menu has the Gluten Free Pizza and the Cauliflower Gluten Free Pizza.

Guests rate Bonbino's 4.5 stars across 395+ Google reviews. “It's a traditional Italian eatery, not fancy but delicious food.” (AvCheu, Google review) “The energy is positive and the food is amazing!” (Aurora Achong, Google review).

You can eat in, take out or order delivery. You can order ahead online and pick it up at the restaurant. We deliver within about 3 miles for a $3 fee with a $1 minimum. There's a free parking lot and free street parking. Stop by 605 Merrick Rd, or call (516) 764-9237 and we'll have your order ready.

## Before you visit

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We don't have outdoor seating, and dogs aren't allowed. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.
